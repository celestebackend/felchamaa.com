Until
Peace

Fadi Elchamaa and Amer Chamaa
Ongoing collaborative painting

2 × 7.5 metres

Ongoing
Intentionally unfinished

A father and son working side by side on a painting that will remain unfinished until true, lasting peace is achieved in the Middle East.

Not a pause in fighting, not a fragile ceasefire, but peace built on dignity, justice and the shared hope for a good future for all people in the region.

The work is a dialogue between generations, between memory and possibility. As it grows, layer by layer, it carries questions: What does it mean to wait for peace? What histories are inherited between parent and child? Can art hold space for reconciliation, healing and perhaps even forgiveness?
