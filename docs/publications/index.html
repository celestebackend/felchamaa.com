What Remains

A Practice, 1982–2026

An autobiographical monograph about the freedom to change and stay the same.

While writing this monograph, I focused on how my work developed rather than on what I felt making it or what others might feel seeing it. Talking about emotion in painting is like talking about being wet in water.
